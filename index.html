Encode small, upscale to 4K

My bachelor thesis asked one question. If you shrink a 4K video to save bandwidth, can a neural network rebuild it better than plain bicubic scaling?

For the two small networks I tested, no. Bicubic stayed ahead on VMAF at every resolution and every compression level.

Starting resolution matters most

Pick the highest resolution your bitrate can fill. At 344 kbps, 720p reached VMAF 41.5. At 321 kbps, 1080p reached only 38.7, because it had too few bits for its pixels.

The networks stayed behind bicubic

The gap ran from under 1 VMAF point at the lowest bitrate to about 5–6 points at the highest, and up to about 9 points at 540p. PSNR makes the gap look larger than VMAF does.

Wrong training data

FSRCNN and ESPCN learned to undo clean bicubic downscaling. They received HEVC-compressed frames and partly sharpened the artefacts.

Bicubic has a head start

The small versions were made by roughly bicubic scaling, so bicubic upscaling recovers most of the low-frequency content.

Old, tiny models

Both date from 2016 and have only a few thousand parameters. Newer models are built for compressed video.

The first result was wrong

The first run showed bicubic ahead by about 9 dB, with network PSNR stuck near 33 dB across a 27× bitrate range. That looked too good to be true, so I checked the measurement itself.

A second lossy encode

OpenCV saved each network output with mp4v, a compression step the bicubic path never went through. Frames now go straight into the scorer.

Two colour conventions

OpenCV used BT.601 while the reference used BT.709. PSNR reacted strongly to this, and VMAF barely moved.

Fixes I tried and rejected
  • Convert network output to BT.709PSNR got worse, because it does not undo OpenCV's BT.601 decode.
  • Decode the reference through OpenCV tooVMAF fell to about 40 even for bicubic, because frame pairing broke.
  • Use cv2.resize for bicubicAbout 6 dB lower than FFmpeg bicubic, because its sampling grid is misaligned with the reference.

Pick the highest resolution your bitrate can fill

  • 1080pBest above about 0.5 Mbps.
  • 720pUseful only around 0.3–0.5 Mbps.
  • 540pNever the best choice, 720p beat it throughout.
  • 270pUnusable for 4K, with VMAF between 0 and 3.2.

Next step: modern models built for compressed video, such as Real-ESRGAN and SwinIR, running on a GPU.
